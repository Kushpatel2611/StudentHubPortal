About CHARUSAT

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) is located in Changa, Gujarat. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes in different fields.

About this StudentHub Portal

This portal is a college project made to bring common student information into one place. Students can browse campus information, check events, view a sample profile and use the registration and feedback pages.

The pages use HTML and CSS for the layout, JavaScript for interactive parts, and JSON files for sample event, student and FAQ data.

What you can find here

  • Student dashboard and profile details
  • Upcoming campus events
  • Student directory with search options
  • Frequently asked questions and feedback

Project note

This is a front-end demonstration. The displayed student details are sample data, and forms are not connected to a university database.
